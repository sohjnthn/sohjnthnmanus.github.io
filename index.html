Mesos Earning Methods (Non-@Cash)

Primary Systems

Ardentmill Extraction
Extract unused equipment via Alchemy to get item crystals. These crystals have value in the Maple Auction.
Daily Bossing
Defeat daily bosses to collect Glowing Soul Crystals. Sell these directly to the NPC Collector in Ardentmill for steady Mesos.
Maple Auction
Sell item crystals and leftover materials for steady Mesos. Check market prices before listing to maximize profit.

Recommended Routine

  1. Train on level-appropriate maps with stable mob density
  2. Extract all unused equipment via Ardentmill
  3. Complete daily bosses for Glowing Soul Crystals
  4. Sell materials and crystals on Maple Auction
  5. Repeat consistently for reliable income

Bossing Guide by Damage Range

Progress through bosses as your damage range increases. Prioritize routine completion over chasing difficulty before your damage is ready.

Tier 1: Entry Level

Damage Range: 0.6m – 1.0m clean damage
Boss: Normal Zakum

Tier 2: Early Expedition

Damage Range: 2.0m+ lower clean damage
Bosses: Normal Hilla, Normal Ranmaru, Normal Horntail

Tier 3: Pre-6th Job

Damage Range: 19.5m+ lower clean damage
Bosses: Normal Vellum/Crimson Queen/Pierre/Von Bon

Tier 4: Weekly/Daily Roster (Post-6th Job)

Damage Range: 19.5m+ lower clean damage range with full 5th/6th job rotation
Key Bosses: Hard/Normal Von Leon, Normal/Easy Arkarium, Chaos Horntail, Normal Kawoong, Hard Ranmaru, Chaos/Normal Papulatus, Chaos/Normal Pink Bean, Hard/Normal Magnus, Normal/Easy Cygnus, Hard Hilla, Chaos Vellum/Crimson Queen/Pierre/Von Bon, Normal Princess Nou, and Normal Akechi Mitsuhide
Strategy: Lead each fight with your highest total-damage opener. Save Ascent for survival situations.

Key Strategies for Success

Steady Beats Fast
Consistent daily routines outperform shortcuts. Regular farming, consistent extraction, and checking market prices will build wealth faster than risky tactics.
Focus on Clean Damage Range
Your clean damage range determines which bosses you can defeat. Prioritize reaching the next damage threshold before attempting harder bosses.
Permanent Content is Your Friend
Use permanent content accessory sets (such as Sengoku) for early damage boosts. These are always available and don't expire.
Authentic Symbols Matter
Focus on leveling Authentic Symbols for Cernium and Arcs to level 11. This provides significant clean damage range boost.
Account-Wide Systems Compound
Maple Union and Link Skills strengthen every character on your account. They're free, permanent, and compound with everything else.
Lower-Tier Bosses Still Pay Out
Don't skip lower-tier bosses. They provide reliable Mesos and help build your routine. Consistency matters more than difficulty.
